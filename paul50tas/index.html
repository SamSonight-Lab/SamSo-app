The Paul 50 Bag
BENJOLIN 50.0 · happy birthday Paul!
  1. Inside the bag plays Paul Tas' 50th anniversary band: five instruments on one clock. 一 BASS, 二 LEAD, 三 DRUMS, 四 PAD and 五 CHAOS FX. Fifty knobs in total.
  2. Each instrument has a sequence that picks notes and rhythms: the Benjolin rungler, a Turing machine, Euclid and more. Every instrument can be generated or sample-based.
  3. Make your own instruments: pick ◉ SAMPLE in an instrument, then ● RECORD with the mic (sing, clap, bang the table) or load a sound FILE. The bag trims, levels and loops it for you.
  4. Tap a row to zoom in, drag up/down to turn a knob, swipe sideways for the next page.
  5. Tap the flap for MASTER: tempo (from 0 up to audio rate), key, scale, glue, RING, scenes and CROSS-MOD.
  6. Hit the red HAPPY ACCIDENTINATOR for a brand new band, then tweak it yourself. The slider sets how wild: MUSICAL to CHAOS. DRIFT makes a running patch breathe and transform. 🔓 TEMPO keeps your tempo. PRESETS saves what you found. FREEZE locks all patterns. And the padlock… just try it.
